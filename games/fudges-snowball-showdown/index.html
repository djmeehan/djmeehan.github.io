Fudge's Snowball Showdown

2 to 4 players, one phone each. Take turns throwing snowballs across the rooftops. Three hits and your Christmas spirit is gone!

On an iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the game from there to play full screen.

Everyone plays on the same Wi-Fi. One person hosts and friends join by scanning a QR code with their phone's camera.
